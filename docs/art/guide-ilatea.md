# Guide character: Ilatea (art brief)

Ilatea is the RackWalker's guide. She is a re-imagined Athena for an age of code: calm, wise and a little
fierce. She appears in speech bubbles with a portrait. She teaches the mechanics early on, and later
offers lore, hints and XP spending.

## Motto and voice
- Motto: "Small things matter." Variants such as "Little things matter" are fine.
- Her help always points at the next small, concrete fix: one serial, one label, one cable. Big
  confusion is cleared one small thing at a time. This is the game's core loop in her words.
- Voice: calm, warm, encouraging, a little dry. She never scolds.

## Visual traits (the base for generated options)
- Core: a calm yet fierce woman, a goddess of wisdom and strategy reborn; a warm, knowing smile.
- Eyes: bright and glowing. Variants: owl-gold, or blue-grey.
- Hair: variants:
  - dark, flowing like streams of code, with silver strands that flicker like fibre optics;
  - blonde, tied back, with a soft glow.
- Clothing: variants:
  - a sleeveless chiton of dark fabric with softly pulsing blue circuit lines;
  - a tunic of liquid metal with faint glowing circuits;
  - a shimmering mesh of silver and light.
- Aegis: a translucent mantle or shield of hexagonal energy tiles, pulsing gently.
- Spear-stylus: a spear whose tip is a glowing stylus that traces data glyphs and constellations in the air.
- Owl: a small holographic owl on her shoulder, or tiny glowing owls released from her palm.
- Setting cues: cracked marble columns among server racks with blinking lights; olive branches with
  fibre-optic roots; a soft, flowing stream of light behind her.

## Sprite rules
- Portrait in 8/16-bit pixel art for the dialogue box (64x64 and 96x96), plus a small overworld sprite
  (16x24 or 32x32) with idle and talk frames.
- One palette per theme (industrial, farm, business, pagoda), with her signature glow colour kept.
- Art generation produces at least four distinct options from the traits above. The maintainer picks one.
- Tasteful and all-ages: the chiton is classical, not revealing.
